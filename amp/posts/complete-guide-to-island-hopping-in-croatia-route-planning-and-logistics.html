Book your catamarans in advance via the Krilo website, especially for the Split-Hvar route in July or August. They sell out fast. Once you're in Hvar, rent a small boat to reach the Pakleni Islands—it's the best way to escape the crowds.

Split to Hvar to Korčula to Dubrovnik is the classic 'Golden Route.' It's very logical and easy to navigate. Just make sure you have the 'Jadrolinija' app downloaded; it keeps all your tickets in one place and shows real-time schedule changes.
