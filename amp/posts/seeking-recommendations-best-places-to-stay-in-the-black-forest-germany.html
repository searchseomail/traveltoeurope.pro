We absolutely loved staying in Titisee-Neustadt! The lake is stunning, and there are so many boat rentals and family activities. Hiking trails are abundant, and the town itself is very picturesque. We stayed at a lovely family-run hotel right by the lake. Highly recommend it for a first-time visit.

Freiburg im Breisgau is a fantastic base. It's a vibrant city with a beautiful old town, a famous Münster, and great transport links for day trips. From there, you can easily explore the surrounding Black Forest. We found some great apartment rentals on Airbnb.

For a truly charming experience, consider Gengenbach. It's one of the most well-preserved medieval towns and feels like stepping back in time. The surrounding vineyards are beautiful for walks. We stayed in a quaint Pension and it was perfect.

Triberg is famous for its waterfalls, which are impressive! It's a bit more touristy, but the sheer scale of the falls and the cuckoo clock shops make it a fun stop. We stayed in a hotel within walking distance of the falls and enjoyed the convenience.

Schluchsee is another beautiful lake town, similar to Titisee but perhaps a bit quieter. Great for water sports and surrounded by forests for hiking. We rented a holiday home there and it was ideal for our family.

If you're looking for a more secluded and nature-focused experience, look into the area around Baiersbronn. It's known as a 'gourmet village' but also offers incredible hiking. We stayed in a small guesthouse and felt immersed in nature.

Don't underestimate the smaller villages! We stayed in Hinterzarten, which is charming and has excellent access to hiking and cross-country skiing (though you're going in summer). It's less crowded than Titisee but still has amenities.

We found staying near Baden-Baden to be a good option. It's a bit more upscale with its famous spas, but the surrounding Black Forest hills offer fantastic walks, and it's a good base for exploring the northern parts of the region.
