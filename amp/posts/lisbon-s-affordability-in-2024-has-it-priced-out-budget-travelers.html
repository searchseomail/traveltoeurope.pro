Lisbon's Affordability in 2024: Has it priced out budget travelers?

faded_tide OP
09.03.2025 02:08
I'm planning a trip to Lisbon later this year and remember hearing great things about how affordable it used to be, especially compared to other major Western European cities. However, with the recent surge in tourism and global inflation, I'm starting to worry that its reputation for being 'cheap' might be outdated. For those who've visited recently, how have you found the costs for accommodation, dining out, public transport, and activities? I'm aiming for a mid-range budget, not super luxury but also not hostel-only, and I'd love to hear if it's still possible to have a great experience without breaking the bank.

I visited Lisbon in March 2024, and while it's definitely not as 'cheap' as it was pre-pandemic, it's still very manageable for a mid-range budget. Accommodation saw the biggest jump, so book early! We found charming Airbnb apartments for around €80-€100/night outside the absolute busiest areas. Dining out can still be very affordable if you stick to local tascas and avoid the super touristy spots. A 'prato do dia' (dish of the day) is your best friend!

Just got back last week! You're right, the 'bargain' reputation is a bit outdated, but it hasn't priced out mid-range travelers. We stayed in a lovely boutique hotel in Alfama for €120/night. Meals were generally €15-€25 per person for a nice dinner with wine. Public transport is still incredibly cheap – buy a Viva Viagem card and load it up. It's definitely not the 'super cheap' Lisbon of 5-7 years ago, but it's far from breaking the bank if you're smart. Stay slightly outside the absolute city center, eat at local tascas, and use the metro/tram. Public transport is still incredibly cheap. I was there last month, and while accommodation has certainly jumped (book well in advance!), food and transport are still reasonable. We found great dinners for €15-€20 per person, including wine, away from the main squares. The Lisboa Card is great for museums and unlimited transport. If you're looking for truly off-the-beaten-path and super cheap, maybe look further east, but Lisbon still delivers great value.
