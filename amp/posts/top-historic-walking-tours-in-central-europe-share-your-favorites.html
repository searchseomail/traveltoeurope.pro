For imperial grandeur, Vienna is unparalleled. I took a tour focusing on the Habsburg dynasty and it was incredible. Walking through Schönbrunn Palace and the Hofburg complex with a guide who knew every anecdote about Empress Sisi and Franz Joseph was like stepping back in time. They often have great thematic tours, so keep an eye out for those.
