For genuine therapeutic benefits, you absolutely must consider Hungary. Hévíz has the largest natural thermal lake in the world, and it's an incredible experience for mud baths and mineral soaks. Budapest also has its famous thermal baths like Széchenyi and Gellért, which are fantastic for a cultural spa experience.

The Czech Republic's spa triangle, especially Karlovy Vary, is a classic for a reason. The ornate architecture, the colonnades, and the drinking cures from the mineral springs are truly unique. Mariánské Lázně is another stunning option, often a bit quieter but equally beautiful with excellent therapeutic programs.

Poland has some wonderful, often overlooked, health resorts. Krynica-Zdrój in the Beskid Mountains is known for its mineral waters and beautiful scenery, offering treatments for various ailments. For more specialized mud therapies, Busko-Zdrój is famous for its sulfide-brine waters.

Don't rule out Romania! Sovata is incredible with its heliothermal salt lakes, particularly Bear Lake, known for its therapeutic mud and very salty water, great for rheumatic conditions. Baile Herculane is ancient, dating back to Roman times, offering a blend of history and mineral spring benefits.

Slovakia's Piešťany is world-renowned for its unique sulfuric mud and thermal mineral water, which are particularly effective for musculoskeletal issues. It's a very established spa town with excellent medical facilities and beautiful parks for relaxation.

Bulgaria offers some fantastic value! Velingrad is often called the 'spa capital of the Balkans' with numerous hotels offering mineral pools and a wide range of treatments. Sandanski, known for its unique climate and mineral waters, is excellent for respiratory problems and general wellness.
