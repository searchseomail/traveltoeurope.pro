Yes, it is definitely possible! Skip Positano for your base and look at staying in Salerno instead. It’s much cheaper, has a real local vibe, and you can easily take the ferry or bus to the more expensive towns for day trips.

I stayed in Vietri sul Mare and saved a fortune on accommodation. If you are planning to travel around the country by train, you might want to check if an Interrail pass makes sense for your broader itinerary, though for just the coast, the local SITA buses are your best bet.

Eat where the locals eat! Avoid restaurants with English menus right on the main squares. Look for 'pasticcerias' for breakfast and 'tavola calda' spots for lunch—you can get a delicious meal for under 10 euros.

Stay in Sorrento. It’s a great transport hub for the entire region, and because it’s larger, there is way more competition for hotels, keeping prices much more reasonable than in the tiny cliffside villages.

Don't bother with private boat tours. Use the public ferries to travel between Amalfi, Positano, and Minori. It’s significantly cheaper and gives you the exact same stunning views from the water.
