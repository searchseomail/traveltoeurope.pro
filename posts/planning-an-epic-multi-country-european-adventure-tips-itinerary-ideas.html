That's an exciting trip to plan! For balancing travel time, I'd suggest grouping countries geographically. For example, focus on Western Europe (France, Belgium, Netherlands) or Central/Eastern Europe (Czech Republic, Austria, Hungary). Trains are usually fantastic for this, and you can often get overnight options to save on accommodation and travel time. Check out sites like Omio or Trainline to compare prices. Great question! My biggest tip is to pick a 'hub' city and do day trips or shorter stays in nearby countries. For instance, base yourself in Berlin and explore Prague and Budapest. For a multi-country trip, I always recommend looking at a route that flows naturally. Like, start in Portugal, move up through Spain, then France, and maybe Switzerland. This minimizes backtracking. Trains are generally the most scenic and convenient way to travel between many European cities.
