The Eurail pass is often overrated for short trips. If you know your dates in advance, booking point-to-point tickets on national rail websites like SNCF or Deutsche Bahn is almost always cheaper. Just make sure to book at least 4-6 weeks out.

Night trains are a lifesaver for your budget! You save on a hostel night, but remember that many require a seat reservation fee even with a pass. Check out the ÖBB Nightjet network; it’s reliable and covers a good chunk of Central Europe.
