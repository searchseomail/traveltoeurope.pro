Setting up an eSIM is usually pretty simple. Most providers will send you a QR code via email. You'll go into your phone's settings, find the cellular/mobile data section, and select 'Add eSIM'. Then, you scan the QR code, and it guides you through the rest. Do this *before* you leave home to avoid any issues!
